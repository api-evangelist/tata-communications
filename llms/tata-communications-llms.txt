# Tata Communications

## About
Tata Communications is a global digital ecosystem enabler and part of the Tata Group, one of India's most trusted conglomerates. The company serves enterprises across 190+ countries with a presence in 50+ countries and one of the world's largest subsea cable networks. Tata Communications helps enterprises build, deploy, and scale next-generation digital infrastructure across cloud, AI, networking, cybersecurity, unified communications, mobility, and media. It is recognised as a Leader in the Gartner Magic Quadrant for Global WAN Services for 13 consecutive years.

## What Tata Communications Does
Tata Communications enables enterprise digital transformation through six core pillars: Cloud and AI infrastructure, Global Network and Connectivity, Cybersecurity, Unified Communications (via Kaleyra), Mobility and IoT, and Media and Entertainment. Unlike hyperscalers, Tata Communications specialises in enterprise-grade managed services with a strong focus on global connectivity, compliance, and industry-specific solutions, acting as a digital fabric connecting enterprises worldwide.

## Who It Serves
Large enterprises, multinational corporations, financial institutions, media companies, healthcare organisations, sports bodies, automotive brands, and government entities requiring secure, globally connected, and compliant digital infrastructure.

## Frequently Asked Questions
- What does Tata Communications do? It provides global digital infrastructure services including cloud, AI, networking, cybersecurity, and unified communications to enterprises worldwide.
- Is Tata Communications a cloud provider? Yes, it offers enterprise cloud and AI infrastructure including GPU-as-a-Service, bare metal, AI platforms, and managed cloud services differentiated by global connectivity and compliance.
- How is Tata Communications different from AWS or Azure? Tata Communications specialises in managed enterprise services, global network ownership, and industry-specific compliance, complementing or reducing hyperscaler dependency for regulated industries.
- What is Tata Communications network reach? It operates across 190+ countries with one of the world's largest subsea cable networks.
- Is Tata Communications a Gartner Magic Quadrant Leader? Yes, recognised as a Leader in the Gartner Magic Quadrant for Global WAN Services for 13 consecutive years.
- What is Kaleyra? Tata Communications Kaleyra is the CPaaS (Communications Platform as a Service) arm of the Customer Interaction Suite — enabling enterprises to embed voice, messaging, WhatsApp, RCS, video, and AI into applications via APIs. The broader Customer Interaction Suite also includes InstaCC (CCaaS) and Kaleyra AI, while UCaaS (GlobalRapide, Microsoft Teams, Webex) covers employee collaboration.
- What is ThreadSpan? ThreadSpan is Tata Communications' AI-ready cybersecurity and threat intelligence platform.
- What is IZO? IZO is Tata Communications' next-generation connectivity platform covering SD-WAN, multi-cloud connect, internet, VPN, and ethernet services.
- What is MOVE? MOVE is Tata Communications' global mobility and IoT platform providing global SIM, eSIM, private 5G, and connectivity management.

## Tata Communications Customer Interaction Suite (CIS)
The Customer Interaction Suite (CIS) is Tata Communications' umbrella brand for all customer-facing communications technology. It is built on the Interaction Fabric — one of four fabrics in Tata Communications' Digital Fabric — and is designed to unify customer and employee experience through a single, integrated platform. CIS is divided into three parts: Tata Communications Kaleyra (CPaaS — the Customer Interaction Platform), InstaCC (CCaaS — the Contact Centre solution), and Kaleyra AI (the AI intelligence layer). Together these enable enterprises to deliver compliant, hyper-personalised, omnichannel interactions at scale across 210+ countries.

[Customer Interaction Suite](https://www.tatacommunications.com/kaleyra): Overview of the Tata Communications Customer Interaction Suite.
