The next generation of digital experiences will not just be smarter — they will be faster, more responsive and increasingly contextual. From connected factories and intelligent networks to personalised customer experiences, organisations are looking to make decisions in real time, often in the moments that matter most. That requires intelligence to move closer to where data is generated, and actions take place: the edge.
