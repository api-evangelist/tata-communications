What if a Network Operations Centre (NOC) could detect an incident, understand its impact, determine the right response, execute approved actions, and confirm recovery before an engineer opens the first ticket? That is the evolving promise of the Dark NOC. Gartner predicted that by 2026, 30% of enterprises would automate more than half of their network activities , up from under 10% in mid-2023.
