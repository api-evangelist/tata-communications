The workplaces of today are undergoing significant shifts, thanks to rapid technological advancements, fast evolving business models, and the rise of multigenerational teams. In such an environment, agility is no longer optional, rather a business imperative. Traditional hierarchical structures, once the backbone of organisational design, are steadily giving way to more fluid systems that prioritise speed, collaboration and adaptability.
